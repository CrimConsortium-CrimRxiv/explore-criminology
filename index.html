Explore Criminology.

The CrimRxiv Consortium is a group of universities, libraries, and organizations that sustain CrimRxiv and the public resources around it. It works to make criminology research free to read, share, and reuse.

Open-access hub & repository

CrimRxiv

CrimRxiv is the global open-access hub and repository for criminology. It is where researchers freely share and find papers — preprints, working papers, and postprints — alongside data, code, and preregistrations from across the field.

Provided by the University of Manchester and Knowledge Futures, and sustained by the CrimRxiv Consortium.
