Explore Criminology.

CrimConsortium is an international group of institutions and individuals who make criminology research open — free to read, share, and reuse. It increases the quantity, quality, and use of open-access articles, reports, books, teaching materials, and other outputs. This website directs visitors to what it makes and supports, the flagship of which is CrimRxiv.

Hub & repository on PubPub

CrimRxiv

CrimRxiv is the global open-access hub and repository for criminology. It is where you freely share and find papers and other resources.

Provided by the University of Manchester and Knowledge Futures, and sustained by CrimConsortium.

Permanent decentralized mirror

CrimRxiv Archive

A permanent, decentralized archive of CrimRxiv on Arweave. Every resource carries a cryptographic identifier and is stored forever — independent of any single platform or institution. The archive is updated periodically.

A pilot of the AR.IO Continuum digital-preservation initiative.
